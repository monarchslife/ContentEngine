07 · MethodAnd its limits

Method and limits

Each student takes the Three-Minute Test on Day 1 and again at the end: a 60-second introduction, a 90-second story and a 30-second curveball, recorded after a room check. Answers are scored on the Offer Score: Structure, Proof, Clarity, Presence and Fit, each from 1 to 4, for a total of 5 to 20. Claude scores Structure, Proof, Clarity and Fit from the transcripts and measured delivery; a coach reviews every score, scores Presence from the video, and logs a reason for any change.

What this report does not claim. These scores measure interview readiness on our rubric. They are not a prediction or guarantee of placement, which depends on the companies that visit, the seats they bring and eligibility rules.
