Three-Minute Test

Okay, here's the deal.

  • 3 questions, one at a time. 60 seconds, then 90, then 30.
  • Recording starts on its own after a 5-second countdown. Talk till the timer runs out, or tap Done if you finish early.
  • No retakes. Real interviews don't have them either. Honestly, your first try tells us way more than a polished tenth try.
  • Messing up is part of the plan. Day 1 is supposed to be a bit rough. That's what makes the Final look so good.

Two-minute setup

  • Quiet room. Phone on silent.
  • Earphones with a mic, if you have them.
  • Camera at eye level. Stack some books under the phone, nobody's watching.
  • Light in front of your face, not behind you.

Your recording is only for scoring. Yogesh watches it, scores it, and it gets deleted after your report. It never goes to recruiters or anywhere public.
